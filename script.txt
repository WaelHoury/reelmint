ok, but like, Is a river a “thing,” or an event that keeps happening?
Because here’s the weird part: a river is never the same water twice. That water is gone—downstream, evaporated, in a cloud—yet we still point and say, “That’s the River,” like it’s one object.
So what are we actually naming?
The water? That changes every second.
The shape? That shifts with every storm.
The flow pattern? That’s the closest thing to a “same.”
Maybe a river is less like a thing… and more like a verb.
Not something that is, but something that keeps happening.
And if that’s true… when you stand at the bank and say “this is the river”… are you naming a thing—
or just catching an event in the act?
