ok, but like… is cereal soup?
Because think about the definition: soup is stuff… floating in liquid… that you eat with a spoon.
And cereal is literally crunchy things floating in milk. Spoon included.
But then people go, “No, soup is hot.”
Okay… gazpacho exists. Cold soup.
“So it has to be savory.”
Fine… fruit soup exists too.
So what’s the real rule?
Is soup about temperature? Flavor?
Or is it just… chunks in a liquid?
And if that’s the case… when you pour milk over cereal…
did you make breakfast—
or did you accidentally make soup?
